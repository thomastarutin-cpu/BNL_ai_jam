Laser Type Key 

SS = Solid-state bulk laser 
Crystal or glass gain medium. Includes Ti:sapphire, Nd:glass, Nd:YAG, Yb:YAG, etc. This is the main class for most petawatt laser facilities. 

OP = Optical parametric laser 
Uses nonlinear optical parametric amplification rather than a conventional laser gain medium. Includes OPA and OPCPA systems. 

HY = Hybrid solid-state / parametric laser 
Uses both optical parametric amplification and solid-state amplification in the main high-power chain. Use this for systems where OPCPA is central but the final system is not purely parametric. 

GAS = Gas laser 
Gas-phase gain medium. Includes CO2 lasers and excimer lasers. 

FIB = Fiber laser 
Rare-earth-doped optical fiber gain medium. Includes Yb-fiber, Er-fiber, Tm-fiber, etc. 

DIO = Semiconductor / diode laser 
Semiconductor gain medium with direct electrical injection. Also includes diode-laser systems used as primary sources, not merely pump diodes. 

FEL = Free-electron laser 
Radiation generated by relativistic electron beams in undulators. Includes XUV and X-ray free-electron lasers. 

DYE = Dye laser 
Organic dye solution gain medium. Mostly historical or tunable visible systems. 

CHEM = Chemical laser 
Laser powered by chemical reactions, e.g. HF/DF chemical lasers. 

UNK = Unknown / not yet classified 
Use only temporarily when the source does not make the laser type clear. 

Recommended database columns: 

Laser_Type_Code: SS, OP, HY, GAS, FIB, DIO, FEL, DYE, CHEM, or UNK 

Laser_Subtype: more specific description, e.g. Ti:sapphire CPA, Nd:glass CPA, all-OPCPA, OPCPA + Ti:sapphire, OPCPA + Nd:glass, CO2, excimer, Yb-fiber, XFEL 

For our current petawatt survey, the dominant codes will be: 

SS = Ti:sapphire and Nd:glass petawatt systems 
OP = all-OPCPA systems 
HY = OPCPA + Ti:sapphire or OPCPA + Nd:glass systems 
GAS, FIB, DIO, FEL = extension categories for broader wavelength coverage
